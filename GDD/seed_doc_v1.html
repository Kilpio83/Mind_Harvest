Visual Novel — Seed Document v0.1

The Couch

Working title

The kernel

A newly graduated therapist — inexperienced, textbook-trained, quietly lost — begins seeing patients whose desires and compulsions are far outside anything he studied. Each session strips away another layer of his carefully constructed professional identity. He is supposed to be helping them find themselves. He finds himself instead.

Structural engine

Isekai framing — the protagonist enters a world that operates on rules he doesn't understand yet. The unknown world is adult human psychology. Each patient is a region of that world with its own logic, language, and pull. The player discovers it alongside him.

Audience

Adult players who use interactive fiction as a safe container for fantasy. SillyTavern RP crowd. DeviantArt sensibility. People who want to feel something real, not just consume content.

Intended effect

Satisfaction. Surprise. Fun. The player should feel tested — on the same journey as the protagonist — discovering preferences and limits they didn't know they had.

Tone target

Fun on the surface. Real underneath. With teeth. Playful enough that no one feels lectured. Deep enough that something actually lands.

Protagonist arc

Denial → exposure → reckoning → (player-determined) acceptance or consequence. The arc is his, but the destination is theirs. Multiple endings carry equal weight — no "correct" path.

Open questions (living)

  • Protagonist name, age, specific background
  • Practice setting — private, inherited, shared?
  • Number of patient routes at launch
  • How far each patient route pushes the protagonist
  • Where ethical lines sit within the fiction
